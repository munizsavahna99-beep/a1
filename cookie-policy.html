Cookie Policy

How cookies and similar technologies may be used.

Essential operation

Basic technologies may support security, preferences and reliable page delivery. Where consent is legally required, non-essential analytics storage should remain disabled until a visitor chooses otherwise.

Analytics

Google Analytics 4 can help us understand aggregate page use. Consent Mode communicates the visitor’s storage choice. Browser settings and privacy extensions may provide additional controls.

Your choices

You can clear or block cookies in your browser. Blocking essential storage may affect some preferences, but the main editorial pages are designed to remain readable. We will update this page if the technologies we use materially change.
