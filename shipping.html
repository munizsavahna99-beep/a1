Shipping Policy

Our current position on delivery and physical goods.

No physical fulfilment

This website is an editorial journal. It does not currently accept orders for ingredients, cookware, printed publications or other physical items, so no shipping charges, delivery areas or estimated arrival dates apply.

External references

A future article may mention a third-party product for context. Any purchase, delivery or return would be governed by that retailer’s displayed terms. A reference does not imply that we fulfil or guarantee the item.

Future changes

If physical goods become available, we will publish clear destinations, costs, dispatch estimates, carrier limitations, tracking availability and lost-or-damaged parcel procedures before checkout is enabled.
