Editorial Disclaimer

Important limits around recipes, nutrition and food safety.

General information

Our content is culinary editorial, not medical, nutritional or allergy advice. Individual needs differ. Consult a qualified professional for guidance relating to health conditions, pregnancy, allergies or therapeutic diets.

Kitchen safety

Follow current official guidance for hygiene, internal temperatures, cooling, storage and reheating. Check labels every time because formulations can change. Prevent cross-contact when serving anyone with an allergy.

No guaranteed result

Equipment, ingredients, skill and environment affect cooking. Descriptions are guidance, not promises of taste, appearance, timing or suitability. Use judgement and manufacturer instructions, particularly with heat, knives and electrical appliances.
