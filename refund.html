Refund Policy

Why refunds do not currently apply to this editorial website.

No direct sales

Savory Supper Journal currently provides free editorial content and does not sell meals, subscriptions, downloads or physical goods through this website. We therefore do not take website payments and there is ordinarily nothing to refund.

If a charge appears

Do not send payment details through the website. If you believe a third party has used our name in connection with a charge, contact your bank or payment provider promptly and report the details to us using the contact page.

Future paid offerings

If we introduce a paid product or service, its price, fulfilment, cancellation and refund terms will be shown clearly before purchase. Those specific terms will take priority for that transaction, alongside applicable consumer law.
