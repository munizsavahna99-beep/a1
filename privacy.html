Privacy Policy

We explain what limited information this editorial website may process.

Information and purpose

This site does not create user accounts or process orders. Basic technical data may be processed to keep pages secure and understand aggregate usage. If you contact us, your email provider handles the message and we use the details only to respond.

Analytics and consent

Google Analytics 4 is configured with Consent Mode. Analytics storage remains denied unless a visitor grants permission through an applicable consent interface. Google may process data under its own terms. You can use browser controls to limit cookies.

Sharing, retention and rights

We do not sell personal information. Service providers may process limited data for hosting, security or analytics under their own safeguards. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information we hold, subject to legal limits. Retention depends on purpose and applicable law.

Children and changes

The site is general-audience editorial content and is not directed to children. We may update this notice when site functionality or legal requirements change. The date shown below identifies the current version.
